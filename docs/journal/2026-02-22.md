The convergence gate requires phase 6 + 1M steps before the pipeline transitions to self-play. At this rate, phase 2 at 21.7M steps means the pool training will likely exhaust its 30M step budget without reaching phase 6. When that happens, the pipeline will exit and self-play will need to be launched manually from the best pool model.

This is the scaling wall noted yesterday — the 81-cell board with 163 actions per step makes exploration dramatically harder. Size 8 cleared all phases by 3M steps; size 9 is still working through phase 2 at 21.7M. May need to revisit the convergence criteria or increase the step budget for larger sizes.
